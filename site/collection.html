How do I get my_cards.csv?

It is the same file the archive app's 10) Show only my cards uses. Open The Fifth Line, search your wallet address to open your portfolio, then click Export my cards. Any CSV works if it has a card_id, url, or series_id + token_id column (an edition column is optional). A plain text file with one card number or NHL Breakaway link per line works too.
