What this site is

This is an unofficial fan preservation archive for NHL Breakaway moments. Fans built it so you can still find and enjoy the cards if the original site goes away.

  • It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NHL, NHLPA, NHLAA, Sweet, or any team.
  • The site does not host media. Covers and videos load from NHL Breakaway’s own servers first, with public IPFS as a backup.
  • Nothing here asks you to connect a wallet, sign in, or spend money.
Disclaimer. Use at your own risk. There is no warranty that the data is complete or that every file stays available. See the No warranty section in the README.

Browsing the Museum

The Museum lists every known moment. Use it like a catalogue.

Search, filters, and sorting

  • Search by player, team, set, or other text in the search box.
  • Filters narrow the list (for example by team or set when those controls are shown).
  • Sorting changes the order of the grid (newest, name, and so on).

Opening a moment and playing a video

Tap or click a card to open its detail panel. From there you can read the moment info and play the video when a source answers. If playback is slow, give it a few seconds — the page tries Breakaway’s servers first, then IPFS.

What “At risk” means

A moment tagged At risk is one that is not listed on NHL Breakaway right now, or whose IPFS copies look unhealthy or missing. It is a heads-up that the files may be harder to reach — not a guarantee they are gone forever.

Why a cover or video might be slow or missing

  • Media comes from Breakaway’s servers first, with IPFS as backup. Neither lives on this site.
  • Many cards loading at once can make thumbs lag.
  • A cold IPFS file or a temporary outage can leave a blank until you try again later.

For offline copies on your own computer, use the desktop app.

My collection

On My collection you can drop in a CSV of your cards, browse only those moments, save the list in this browser, and share a link. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Getting my_cards.csv from The Fifth Line

  1. Open The Fifth Line and search your wallet address. That opens your portfolio.
  2. Open your portfolio if it is not already open.
  3. Click Export my cards (next to Compress). It downloads my_cards.csv.

Adding cards by hand

You can also build a simple text file yourself. Put one card per line:

  • An NHL Breakaway moment link, or
  • A seriesId and tokenId pair (the same style the app and CSV expect).

Lines that do not match a known card are listed and skipped; nothing else breaks.

Dropping the file in

On My collection, drop the CSV onto the page or use the file picker. Your moments appear in the grid. You can clear the file and start over any time.

Saved collections (this browser only)

  • Use Save in this browser to keep a named list in this browser only (localStorage). Other people and other devices do not see it.
  • Open a saved list and use Export my_cards.csv to keep a backup or move it.
  • To use the same list on another device, export the CSV there (or re-export from The Fifth Line), drop it in again, then save it on that device if you like.

Share links

Use Copy share link to copy a link with your card list encoded in the URL (the #c= part). Anyone who opens it sees that collection. The link can get long for big lists; that is normal. Clearing site data on your phone or browser removes saved collections, but a share link you already copied still works for anyone who has it.

Collector Hall

The Collector Hall is a public wall of fan collections under a handle. A maintainer reviews every submission before it appears.

How to submit

  1. On My collection, load your cards, then click Submit to the Collector Hall. That opens the GitHub form in a new tab.
  2. Drag your my_cards.csv straight into the form’s Cards box (or paste the file’s contents). Pick a handle, not your real name (3–30 lowercase letters, numbers, underscores or dashes; NHL Breakaway usernames are fine).
  3. Submit. A bot checks the list, then a maintainer reviews the pull request. When it is merged, your entry shows up in the Hall.

Details and the form live in CONTRIBUTING.md.

Updating your entry

Submit again from the same GitHub account with the same handle and the updated card list (drag the new CSV in). A maintainer will refresh your entry.

Asking for removal

Open a removal request and say the handle. Maintainers will take it down.

Troubleshooting FAQ

Covers fail with “no source answered” or rate limits

The app politely slows down when Breakaway or a gateway says “too many requests.” Wait about an hour and run the same step again. Files you already saved are skipped, so a second run only retries what is still missing.

A line in my file does not match a card

That line is listed and skipped. Fix the link or ids and drop the file in again. Matching cards still load.

The share link is really long

That is expected. The card list lives in the link itself so nothing is stored on a server. For everyday use on the same phone or computer, Save a named collection instead.

My saved collections disappeared

They live only in this browser’s storage. Clearing site data, using a private window, or switching browsers or devices wipes them. Export a backup CSV when a list matters to you, or keep the share link.

A cover or video is blank on the website

Try again later, or download with the desktop app so you have a local copy. Remember: this site does not host the media.

Privacy

  • CSVs are read in your browser and never uploaded to this project’s servers. Dropping a file or saving a collection stays on your device.
  • Share links put the card list in the URL you copy; only people you give that link to can see it.
  • The Collector Hall only shows what you submit through the public form (handle and card list). Use a handle, not your real name.

Contact and takedowns

Questions, corrections, or a request to remove something: open a GitHub issue. Please include enough detail for maintainers to find the entry (for Hall removals, the handle).

Rights holders who need a takedown can use the same issues page. This is a fan archive with no media hosted here; links and catalogue data are what the site shows.
